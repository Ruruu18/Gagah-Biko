Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account with Gagah Home Made-Biko, we collect the following information:

  • Full name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Shipping address
  • Order history and preferences
  • Payment method type (we do not store GCash wallet details)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order confirmation and updates via email
  • Improve our products and services

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with trusted delivery partners to fulfill your order.

4. Data Security

All transactions are protected with industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. Your personal data is stored securely and accessible only to authorized personnel.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
