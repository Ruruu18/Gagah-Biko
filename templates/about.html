The Gagah Home Made-Biko Story

"Bringing the heart of the fiesta to your table, one sticky slice at a time."

Every tray of Biko you see here isn't just made of rice, coconut milk, and sugar. It's made of teamwork.

In our home, the kitchen is where the hard work happens. While one of us prepares the banana leaves, another is carefully stirring the pot, making sure every grain of rice is coated in that rich, golden caramel. It's a slow process that requires a lot of patience but we don't mind the heat or the tired arms.

Why do we do it? We started this business with a simple goal: to help our family move forward. Every bilao we sell and every tub we deliver goes directly to keeping our lights on, paying the bills, and making sure our needs are met.

When you order from us, you aren't just getting a delicious, traditional treat. You are helping a family stay afloat. You are part of the reason we can keep going.

From our family's kitchen to your table — thank you for tasting the love and supporting our journey.
